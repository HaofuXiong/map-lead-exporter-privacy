Privacy Policy

Extension: Map Lead Exporter
Last updated: 2026-11-18

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Map Lead Exporter browser extension (the "Extension") accesses, why it needs that access, and how the data is handled. The Extension is provided by HF X.

2. How We Handle Data

All extraction and export happen locally in your browser. The Extension only reads publicly visible business information on the map page you open; it never accesses data behind a login, never reads your personal browsing history, and never bypasses captchas or access controls. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension uses no analytics and no advertising. The only network request is paid-license activation/validation via Gumroad (api.gumroad.com), subject to the Gumroad privacy policy. The XLSX export library (SheetJS) is bundled locally — no remote code is loaded.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Extracted leads and settings are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall the Extension, or immediately when you click "Clear" in the dashboard. No merchant data is sent to our servers, so there is nothing server-side to delete. For license-related requests, contact us at the email below.

5. Your Rights (EEA / UK Users)

Under the GDPR or UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to or restrict processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

6. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and where required, announced within the Extension.
